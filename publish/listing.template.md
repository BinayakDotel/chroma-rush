## Description

Chroma Rush is a fast colour-matching endless runner. Race a fox in a go-kart down a twisting road and switch your kart to the colour of each gate before you reach it. Red circle, yellow triangle, blue square: match the gate and you fly through, miss it and you crash.

Gates are only the start. Swerve around rocks, jump over logs and lava, grab fuel before the tank runs dry, and collect gems to fill your Prism meter. When it is full, hit Prism Overdrive for a burst of speed that smashes through everything in your way.

Every run earns shards. Spend them on upgrades and on four more worlds, each with its own driver and vehicle: a penguin on a bobsled in Glacier, a gecko in a dune buggy in Canyon, a rock golem in a monster truck in Volcano, and an alien in a star racer in the Solar System. Missions give every run a goal, and rare power gems (Shield, Magnet, Full Tank, Prism Charge and the Phoenix Gem that revives you) can turn a good run into your best one.

Runs start calm and get faster the further you go. How far can you get?

## How to play

Be the same colour as the gate when you reach it. Change lanes to avoid blocks, jump over low hurdles, and drive through fuel cans and gems to collect them. Fill the Prism meter by passing gates and collecting orbs, then trigger Prism Overdrive.
